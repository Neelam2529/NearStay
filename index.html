Is food included in the monthly hostel rent? ▼
Yes! Over 90% of our listed hostels include 3-time South Indian meals (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner with Sunday specials) in the monthly rent with zero extra charges.
How does the live room grid work? ▼
Property wardens update room statuses directly on NearStay. When you select an available room (Green), it gets locked for you with an instant digital booking confirmation pass.

No! You can book and hold your room with ₹0 platform fee. You pay the rent and security deposit directly to the hostel warden at the time of physical check-in.
