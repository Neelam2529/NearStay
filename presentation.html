Current Reality
The Problem: Why Student Accommodation Hunting is Broken

1. Blind & Exhausting Offline Search

Students migrating to college walk 10-15 km in extreme heat checking hostels one-by-one with zero real-time vacancy visibility.

2. Exploitation by Unregistered Brokers

Local offline agents charge 50% to 100% of the first month's rent as heavy brokerage commission without legal guarantees.

3. Outdated Classifieds & Fake Photos

Platforms like OLX and Facebook groups show years-old pictures, dead phone numbers, and uninspected food mess claims.

Our Innovation
The Solution: How NearStay Solves This

⚡ Live Room-Level Availability Matrix

Students can view floor-wise vacant rooms in real-time (Green = Available, Dark = Occupied) and pick their exact room before visiting.

🛡️ 100% Zero-Brokerage Direct Model

Direct connection between students and verified property wardens with ₹0 platform commission or hidden middleman charges.

🍛 Inspected Weekly Food Mess Schedules

Clear 7-day breakfast, lunch, and dinner menus verified on-ground to eliminate food disputes and ensure hygiene.
