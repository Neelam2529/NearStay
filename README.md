## 📌 Executive Summary: What is NearStay & Why Does it Exist?

**NearStay** is an on-ground verified student accommodation and hostel booking platform. It solves the difficulties students and parents face when finding hostels near colleges (e.g. NIT Warangal, Kazipet, KMC, Subedari):

1. **Live Room Availability Grid**: Visual floor-by-floor matrix showing which room is vacant right now.
2. **Weekly Mess Food Schedule**: Transparent 7-day South Indian meal schedule (Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks, Dinner).
3. **Zero Brokerage Guarantee**: Direct connection with authentic wardens without middleman fees.
4. **Official Digital Boarding Pass**: Instant room allocation ticket with QR security badge and 1-click printing.
5. **Warden Self-Listing Portal**: Wardens can register properties and toggle room vacancy in real time.

---

## ❓ Why NearStay vs. Google Search / Google Maps?

* **Google** only tells you a building exists on a map with an unverified phone number.
* **NearStay** tells you which room on which floor is vacant right now, what is on the food menu, locks your room with a digital boarding pass, and guarantees zero brokerage.
